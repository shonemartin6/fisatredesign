FISAT SILVER JUBILEE HOMEPAGE REDESIGN

Stack: Plain HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. No framework or build step.

Implementation notes:
- Design system: navy #0B1F3A, silver #C0C0C0 -> #F5F7FA, gold #C9A227, white/light-grey backgrounds, 12px cards.
- Typography: Playfair Display for headings and Inter for body copy, with responsive spacing and WCAG-conscious contrast.
- Jubilee treatment: 25 badge beside the logo, metallic headline shimmer, announcement/countdown band, timeline and one-time confetti.
- Interactions: sticky/shrinking header, mobile menu, scroll reveal, count-up stats, Then/Now slider, tabs, search, story slider and local campus video.
- Missing factual items are clearly marked as placeholders, especially the alumni figure and official celebration date.

Local assets included:
- assets/images/fisat-logo.png
- assets/images/fisat_hero_local.jpg (poster frame generated from the supplied campus video)
- assets/videos/fisat.mp4

Open index.html directly in a browser. No build step is required.
